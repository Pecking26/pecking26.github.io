A little help along the traces

Keep the signal moving.

For game help, bugs, or privacy questions, contact peckingsupport@gmail.com.

Move first. Then flip.

Tap a switch to send Spark along the circuit toward it. Tap again once Spark arrives to change the route. Match circles to the circle output and diamonds to the diamond output. In the final tier, the inverter changes packet values.

When the circuit gets busy

Hold Scan to slow the traffic while Spark keeps moving. Use Pulse near an enemy to interrupt it. You can pause to think, adjust simulation speed in Settings, and replay completed chips for mastery.

Progress and devices

Campaign progress is saved locally. There is no game account or cross-device progress sync in the current build. Reset Progress, clearing app data, or uninstalling may erase it. Your device's backup settings may affect restoration.

Advertising and Premium

The Apple release offers free online play with ads between chips. Premium is a permanent purchase that removes ads and unlocks offline play; open Settings → Premium & Restore Purchases. Availability depends on your app version and the App Store. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used to buy Premium. First purchase and restoration need internet; verified owners can then play offline. Campaign progress remains local and is separate from Premium ownership. TestFlight and Simulator use test advertising. Privacy choices are available in Settings when required. Ads never interrupt an active circuit.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, operating-system version, game version, chip number, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, payment details, or store account credentials.
