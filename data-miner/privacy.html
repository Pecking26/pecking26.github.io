Privacy policy.

Effective 21 September 2026

Data Miner is developed by Pecking26. This policy covers the mobile game, its development builds, this website, and support communications. Contact peckingsupport@gmail.com with questions or privacy requests.

Game progress

The game stores completed chips, mastery results, earned in-game Data, upgrades, selected abilities, settings, and advertising timing counters on your device. In-game “Data” is a game currency. We do not operate a server that receives your saved campaign. Device or operating-system backups may include app data according to your backup settings.

The current game has no player accounts, social features, separate analytics service, or cross-device save service. It does not request contacts, microphone, camera, or precise location access.

Advertising and consent

Native mobile builds integrate Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Development builds use test ads. Google may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads, maintain the service, and prevent fraud. Data collection can depend on platform settings and consent. Non-personalized ads may still involve device storage and data processing.

Where required, the game presents Google's consent choices before requesting ads and exposes an Advertising privacy choices action in Settings. You can also manage advertising identifiers through your device settings. The current integration requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking authorization.

Google processes information under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Advertising may involve processing outside your country. We do not send your saved campaign or support emails to AdMob.

Purchases

Purchases are not available in the current test build. A permanent ad-free/offline upgrade is planned. Any future store purchase will be processed by Apple or Google; we will not receive your card details. This policy will be updated to describe purchase verification and retained entitlement information before that feature launches.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond, investigate issues, and maintain relevant support records. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. Support messages are kept as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations; you can request deletion.

This website

This website uses GitHub Pages. It has no advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses to provide and secure hosting. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices

You can reset campaign progress in the game or remove locally stored app data through your device settings. Privacy choices managed by Google may be separate from campaign progress. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information, or withdraw consent. Contact us for information we hold; Google and Apple provide controls for information they hold.

Children and changes

The game does not include accounts or player-to-player communication. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it. We will update this page when features or data practices change and show the revised effective date.
