Privacy policy

Effective 23 September 2026. This policy covers the Laser Kiwi Android game, package nz.pecking26.laserkiwi, provided by Project Pecking Order (Pecking26).

Your game progress

Stages, seeds, training, scores and settings are saved on your device. No game account is required. We do not operate a server that receives your saved game, and the game does not ask for your name, date of birth, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Android may back up app data according to your device and Google account settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local save; a device backup may restore it. Review Android backup settings to manage those copies.

Advertising

Laser Kiwi integrates Google AdMob to show interstitial ads between completed stages. The closed-testing build requests test ads. The Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud and keep services working. Google receives this data. Test ads do not imply that no network data is processed.

Our current requests use non-personalized ads, a general-audience ad-content limit, and under-age-of-consent treatment for all players. We do not collect age profiles. These settings limit personalization but do not eliminate all advertising data processing. We request consent information before loading ads; where Google requires a form, it is shown before requesting ads.

Read Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services. Ad SDK data is encrypted in transit. Google controls retention of data it receives under its policies; we cannot identify or directly erase an individual player's ad-service records.

Your choices

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices to review any privacy options required for your region. If no form is available, the game explains that ads use non-personalized, age-restricted requests. You can manage or delete your advertising ID through Android's privacy settings. The core game works offline; network ads cannot load while you are offline. You can continue playing when an ad is unavailable.

Young players

The game is intended for teens aged 13 and over and adults. It contains fantasy combat against animal pests and mild frightening imagery. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Advertising requests receive the restrictive treatment described above regardless of a player's age.

Support and deletion requests

If you email peckingsupport@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that correspondence to respond and resolve your request. Do not include passwords or sensitive information. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence; we retain it only as needed to handle support or meet applicable obligations. The app has no server-side game account to delete.

Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change, including before enabling materially different advertising or account features.
