DEFENDER OF THE NATIVE BUSH

Small bird.
Serious firepower.

Become Super Kiwi. Move sideways, blast approaching pests, gather food and choose powerful upgrades. Protect the native bush through endless stages and boss encounters.

Laser Kiwi is available to invited Android closed testers. A public download is not available yet.

Help with the game

Drag left or right to aim. Your lasers fire automatically. Collect food before it fades, choose an upgrade when you level up, and move out of amber boss attack warnings before they strike. Mana is your health.

Progress saves locally after stage results and training purchases. Do not uninstall the app if you want to keep that progress. Closed-test advertising uses test ads between completed stages and an optional second-chance ad. Complete one to restore 30% mana once per attempt; if you lose again, start a fresh attempt.

For feedback or support, email peckingsupport@gmail.com. Include your device model, Android version and a description of the problem.
